## Control-flow implications

**The stuck-loop threshold effectively fires on two consecutive identical results in `main`.** The configured window is three entries, and the detector takes two entries from `state.history` before appending the current error. Since `main` has already appended the current result, that error is counted twice. Thus two consecutive `CODEX_ERROR` results produce a three-item list of the same error. `_route_stuck_loop` sends repeated `CODEX_ERROR` to `FATAL`; only repeated test, spec-conflict, and validation errors take its repair/regenerate branch. This follows from the append-before-route order and the detector’s slice-and-append logic.
